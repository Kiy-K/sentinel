# Independent concept proposals — raw digest (2026-11-24)

12 concepts from 3 blind generators + the Sentinel incumbent. Scores as self-reported by generators (not yet independently scored).

## Incumbent

- **Sentinel v0 — generic agentic personal assistant.** Incumbent; no differentiating wedge identified. Judges will have seen many of these.

## Generator A (inventive)

- **A1 Gravekeeper** — agent as executor of a dead person's digital estate; reconstructs projects, owes, wishes; voice-narrated memory book. Weird Uncle lane. Orig 9 / Wow 9. Risk: tone + fictional-data demo.
- **A2 Cold Caller** — AI phones vendors to negotiate bills/cancel subs. Wow 9, orig 8. Risk: telephony rails unavailable/unapproved; latency.
- **A3 The Apprentice** — learns your job from your history, drafts-as-you with confidence scores. Orig 8 / Wow 8. Risk: indistinguishability eval.
- **A4 Spright** — Tamagotchi whose physiology is your prod system; lives on the sprite. Orig 9 / Wow 10. Most sprite-native. Weird Uncle w/ grand-prize upside.

## Generator B (weird-but-useful)

- **B1 Estate Executor** — dead-man's switch; drills "still alive?", escalates to family with briefing URL. Orig 8 / Wow 8, Grand lane.
- **B2 Haggler** — negotiates bills/refunds over real email; reports savings. Orig 7 / Wow 9, Grand lane. Real ROI story.
- **B3 Night Shift** — background jobs while you sleep; Weird Uncle.
- **B4 Grudge Ledger** — social-accountability ledger for flaky friends. Orig 9 / Wow 8, Weird Uncle.

## Generator C (skeptical product)

- **C1 Night Shift** — personal on-call engineer: watches your repos, triages CI/issues overnight, emails digest + PRs. Grand lane; "prize odds" pick.
- **C2 Receipts Clerk** — closes your books monthly from emailed receipts. Orig 7 / Wow 9? (safest ship).
- **C3 Sentry Parrot** — watchdog for small-print changes (ToS, prices, renewals). Orig ? — watchdog/monitor pattern.
- **C4 Dead Man's Switch, But Useful** — legacy/ops agent. Same family as A1/B1.

## Cross-cutting observations

- Three independent generators converged on **dead-man's-switch / estate executor** (A1, B1, C4) — strong emotional resonance but heavy demo risk and tone control.
- Two converged on **bill negotiation** (A2, B2) — strong ROI story; needs email channel we have (AgentMail), but vendor-email automation skirts ToS/external-write approval (D5).
- **Night Shift** appeared twice (B3, C1) — on-call engineer agent; fits GitHub + email + hibernation perfectly; strong Fly-audience resonance.
- Spright (A4) is the single highest-wow, most sprite-native concept; lane risk: "not a personal agent" criticism.
- No generator produced a *good* case for the generic Sentinel v0.
